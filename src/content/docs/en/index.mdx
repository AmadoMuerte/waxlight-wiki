## What is Waxlight

Waxlight brings everything around Vintage Story into one desktop app: multiple accounts with TOTP/2FA support, side-by-side game versions, isolated instances with their own mods and settings, a built-in ModDB browser, backups, and playtime tracking.

The project is maintained by [AmadoMuerte](https://github.com/AmadoMuerte) with help from contributors. Waxlight is **not affiliated** with the developers of Vintage Story, does not distribute the game, and does not bypass its licensing — a valid Vintage Story account is required to play.
